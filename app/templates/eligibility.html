General Medical Standards
  • Age: 18 to 65 years old.
  • Weight: At least 50 kg (110 lbs).
  • Hemoglobin: Minimum 12.5 g/dL.
  • Interval: Minimum 90 days between whole blood donations.
  • Hydration: Drink plenty of water and eat a light meal before donation.
Why Donate?

Every 2 seconds, a patient requires blood for life-saving trauma care, organ transplants, cancer chemotherapy, or complicated childbirth.

Did you know? A single donation of 450 ml can be separated into red cells, plasma, and platelets to save up to three lives!
